## 1. Plan names, monthly / yearly / one-time prices, and annual-billing discount

### Takeaway
Only FieldLevel publishes a full monthly + annual price list: Silver $29/$278, Gold $49/$470, Platinum $79/$758. Its annual plans work out to about a 20% discount. ### Inferences
- The market splits into two models. One is self-serve subscriptions at roughly $20-$80/month (FieldLevel, CaptainU, BeRecruited), with CaptainU's $199.95 Platinum as the high end. The other is sales-led packages (NCSA at about $1,200-$6,000+, SportsRecruits at about $400-$500/yr, often club-bundled). A startup priced at about $10-$30/month undercuts every published monthly price except BeRecruited's lifetime offer.
- FieldLevel's ~20% annual discount is the only clearly published benchmark for monthly vs. annual billing in this set. SportsRecruits' reported $99/mo vs. $399/yr (if accurate) is a much steeper ~66% discount, which pushes buyers toward annual plans.
- BeRecruited's $99.99 lifetime price anchors a "pay once" expectation at a low price. It looks like a legacy offer that has not changed since about 2019.
- NCSA's prices are wide, and quotes in the same tier vary, for example Champion at $1,199 vs. $1,386 vs. $1,800. That fits individually negotiated pricing set on sales calls.

## 2. What the free tier includes and limits

### Takeaway
Every platform offers a free profile. The main paywall is the ability to start messages to coaches, plus, on most platforms, seeing which coaches viewed you. FieldLevel's free plan has no time limit, but free athletes cannot message coaches first. SportsRecruits' free plan has unlimited video but no in-platform messaging. NCSA marks messaging as "Limited" on free. CaptainU's free Bronze plan starts with a 2-week Silver trial.

### Cited Findings
- **FieldLevel free:** The free athlete plan has no time limit. Free athletes cannot message college coaches first, so exposure depends on their own coach promoting them on the platform — [NextCommit FieldLevel review](https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/fieldlevel-review); [FieldLevel Support](https://support.fieldlevel.com/en/articles/2034971-explaining-fieldlevel-s-premium-plans)
- **SportsRecruits free:** includes a recruiting profile builder, unlimited video uploads, a highlight editor, and a school search tool. Per the official page, every message you send includes a tracked profile link, and "Upgrade to Pro to track views, unlock messaging, and more" — [SportsRecruits for Student-Athletes](https://sportsrecruits.com/athletes); [NextCommit SportsRecruits review](https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/sportsrecruits-review)
- **SportsRecruits view alerts are contested:** NextCommit says free accounts get alerts when a college views you. The official page puts coach-view tracking under Pro. A help article for the IWLCARecruits variant (women's lacrosse) says free accounts can "see and track which colleges are viewing your profile, video, and/or transcript, all in real-time." That may not apply to the main platform — [NextCommit](https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/sportsrecruits-review); [SportsRecruits athletes](https://sportsrecruits.com/athletes); [IWLCARecruits help article](https://help.sportsrecruits.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059191811-Whats-the-Difference-Between-a-Free-and-Pro-IWLCARecruits-Account)
- College coaches use SportsRecruits for free — [NextCommit NCSA vs SportsRecruits](https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/ncsa-vs-sportsrecruits)
- **NCSA free:** includes a profile (stats, GPA, test scores, highlight video) and the college search tool/map. NCSA's own pricing table (per snippet) lists the college coach directory and map as included in every tier, and marks "message college coaches" and "message center/activity reports" as "Limited" on Free and "Included" on paid tiers — [NCSA cost page](https://www.ncsasports.org/who-is-ncsa/what-does-ncsa-do/what-does-ncsa-cost-how-much); [GetRecruited: Is NCSA free](https://getrecruited.cc/is-ncsa-free)
- **NCSA coach-view visibility:** coaches can see a free profile — [Rawrecruit](https://rawrecruit.io/resources/compare/is-ncsa-worth-it). However, NCSA's Champion upgrade advertises "See when coaches view your profile", and a third-party guide says detailed profile-activity tracking is not in the free tier — [GetRecruited: Is NCSA free](https://getrecruited.cc/is-ncsa-free); [NCSA](https://www.ncsasports.org/)
- **CaptainU free (Bronze):** the free profile includes a 2-week Silver trial, after which the account reverts to Bronze. Users can cancel at any time by downgrading to Bronze Free — [CaptainU Athlete Plans](https://www.captainu.com/products/athlete); [NextCommit CaptainU review](https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/captainu-review)
- **BeRecruited free:** a free profile exists, and Deluxe is the paid upgrade — [BeRecruited FAQ](https://go.berecruited.com/resources/faq). No details on free-tier limits were retrieved.

### Inferences
- The industry-standard free tier is a profile, video, and school search/directory. The two main reasons to upgrade are (a) contacting coaches first and (b) seeing coach-view or engagement analytics.
- FieldLevel's free plan depends on a coach promoting the athlete, which leaves out athletes without an engaged high school or travel coach. A startup that lets free users send a small number of messages to coaches would stand out against FieldLevel's free plan.

### Gaps
- No exact free-tier message caps (messages per month) were found for any platform. FieldLevel free cannot message first. For NCSA, "Limited" was not quantified.
- CaptainU Bronze limits (whether a free user can email coaches at all, how many, and whether they can see email opens) were not retrieved.
- BeRecruited free-tier limits were not retrieved.
- Whether SportsRecruits free shows coach views is unresolved (sources conflict).

## 3. What paid tiers add

### Takeaway
FieldLevel publishes the clearest feature ladder. Silver adds college activity, matches, and research. Gold adds 20 coach contacts per month (rollover up to 60) plus Needs Alerts. Platinum adds unlimited messages. SportsRecruits Pro adds in-platform messaging and coach-view tracking. NCSA paid tiers add full messaging, view tracking, more video, workshops, and progressively more human recruiting-coach support.

### Cited Findings
- **FieldLevel Silver ($29/mo):** includes College Activity, College Matches, and College Research, but no coach contacts — [NextCommit FieldLevel review](https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/fieldlevel-review); [FieldLevel Support](https://support.fieldlevel.com/en/articles/2034971-explaining-fieldlevel-s-premium-plans)
- **FieldLevel Gold ($49/mo):** Needs Alerts and Contact Colleges are available to Gold and Platinum. Gold athletes get 20 contacts per month, which refresh at the start of each billing cycle. Unused contacts roll over up to a maximum of 60 — [FieldLevel Support: Contact Colleges](https://support.fieldlevel.com/en/articles/1991221-what-is-contact-colleges-and-how-does-it-work) (article is about 909 days old, around mid-2024)
- **FieldLevel Platinum ($79/mo):** unlimited messages to colleges every month — [FieldLevel Support](https://support.fieldlevel.com/en/articles/2034971-explaining-fieldlevel-s-premium-plans)
- **SportsRecruits Pro:** members can message coaches directly through the platform and see which coaches viewed their profile or watched their video — [GetRecruited SportsRecruits review](https://getrecruited.cc/sportsrecruits-review); [SportsRecruits athletes](https://sportsrecruits.com/athletes). The IMLCARecruits (men's lacrosse) Pro tier is described as letting you "connect with every college coach in the country using our SportsRecruits messaging system" — [IMLCARecruits help article](https://help.sportsrecruits.com/hc/en-us/articles/4411390745869-Whats-the-Difference-Between-a-Free-and-Pro-IMLCARecruits-Account)
- Many clubs bundle SportsRecruits Pro into team fees — [NextCommit SportsRecruits review](https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/sportsrecruits-review); [Coastal Field Hockey](https://coastalfieldhockey.com/sports-recruits)
- **NCSA Champion / Elite / MVP / MVP+:** add full coach messaging, "see when coaches view your profile", more highlight-video slots, workshops, and scholarship guidance, with more human recruiting-coach support at each higher tier — [GetRecruited: Is NCSA free](https://getrecruited.cc/is-ncsa-free); [NCSA cost page](https://www.ncsasports.org/who-is-ncsa/what-does-ncsa-do/what-does-ncsa-cost-how-much)
- NCSA may do some messaging or emailing on the family's behalf. A Reddit-focused summary says others pointed out you can do these things yourself — [NextCommit: NCSA reviews](https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/ncsa-reviews)
- Baseball example: on the HS Baseball Web forum, one parent said that when the NCSA baseball coordinator sent out his son's profile, the family saw "an influx of camp invites and questionnaires" (post date not shown) — [HSBaseballWeb: Has anyone used NCSA](https://community.hsbaseballweb.com/topic/has-anyone-ever-used-ncsa-for-recruiting?nc=1)
- **CaptainU paid tiers (Silver/Gold/Platinum):** plan features exist but were not itemized in the retrieved snippets — [NextCommit CaptainU review](https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/captainu-review)

### Inferences
- FieldLevel sets a usage-based benchmark: $49/mo for 20 coach contacts, and $79/mo for unlimited. A startup could set its tier limits on monthly coach-contact counts against these.
- "Who viewed / opened" analytics are paid on NCSA and SportsRecruits (per the official SportsRecruits page). Offering basic open and view tracking on a low-cost tier would be a differentiator.
- Human support (recruiting coordinators/scouts) is what justifies NCSA's four-figure prices. Self-serve platforms do not offer it.

### Gaps
- CaptainU's per-tier features (email limits, email-open tracking, recruiting coordinator on Platinum, and so on) were not retrieved; the official page fetch was blocked.
- What BeRecruited Deluxe adds over free was not retrieved.
- What each NCSA tier includes (for example, how much 1:1 scout time Elite gets vs. MVP) was not retrieved.
- Video-hosting limits per tier were not found for any platform except SportsRecruits free (unlimited uploads).

## 4. Publicly listed vs. hidden prices, and what people report paying

### Takeaway
Publicly listed: FieldLevel (monthly and annual), CaptainU (monthly), BeRecruited (monthly and lifetime). Hidden: SportsRecruits Pro (behind a login; often club-bundled) and all NCSA paid tiers (sales call). Secondhand reports put SportsRecruits at about $99/mo or $399/yr ($250/yr through a club, 2026). NCSA quotes range from about $1,200 to $6,000+, with specific quotes of $1,800 for Champion and $3,000 for MVP. These figures are mostly from competitor blogs and are often undated.

### Cited Findings
- FieldLevel prices appear in a public help-center article — [FieldLevel Support](https://support.fieldlevel.com/en/articles/2034971-explaining-fieldlevel-s-premium-plans)
- CaptainU publishes a "plans start at $22.50/month" price on its athlete page, plus tier prices (labeling inconsistent) — [CaptainU Athlete Plans](https://www.captainu.com/products/athlete)
- BeRecruited publishes $19.99/mo and $99.99 lifetime prices in its FAQ — [BeRecruited FAQ](https://go.berecruited.com/resources/faq)
- SportsRecruits' pricing page sends logged-out visitors to a login screen — [NextCommit SportsRecruits review](https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/sportsrecruits-review). A club source (2026) says $99/mo or $399/yr when registering directly, or $250/yr through the club — [Coastal Field Hockey](https://coastalfieldhockey.com/sports-recruits)
- NCSA asks families to book a call with a recruiting specialist to get pricing — [GetRecruited: How much does NCSA cost](https://getrecruited.cc/how-much-does-ncsa-cost); [NCSA cost page](https://www.ncsasports.org/who-is-ncsa/what-does-ncsa-do/what-does-ncsa-cost-how-much)
- NCSA reported paid amounts (secondhand, see Section 1): $1,800 Champion; $3,000 annual MVP quoted after a 45-minute call; $1,199 Champion / $3,499 MVP one-time; packages of $2,000-$6,000+ reported by families — [GetRecruited.cc](https://getrecruited.cc/how-much-does-ncsa-cost); [GetRecruited.college](https://getrecruited.college/how-much-does-ncsa-cost); [NextCommit: NCSA Cost](https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/ncsa-cost)
- The dated third-party ranges conflict: $1,320-$4,200 (December 2023) vs. $250-$2,100 (October 2025) — [Rawrecruit](https://rawrecruit.io/resources/compare/is-ncsa-worth-it)
- A BBB complaint against NCSA's legal entity, National Collegiate Scouting Association, LLC (Bradenton, FL), says the family owed "30% of the total, which is over $2500" when they cancelled a week after joining. It is ambiguous whether $2,500+ is the 30% or the total — [BBB NCSA complaints](https://www.bbb.org/us/fl/bradenton/profile/athletic-organizations/national-collegiate-scouting-association-llc-0654-7001121/complaints?page=6)

### Inferences
- The hidden-price platforms (NCSA, SportsRecruits) are the ones that sell through sales calls or club deals. Transparent self-serve pricing is itself a positioning angle against them.
- If SportsRecruits charges about $99/mo, a $10-$30/mo startup plan is a 70-90% saving at monthly billing. Against NCSA, even a full year of a premium startup plan would cost a fraction of a single Champion package.

### Gaps
- No Reddit thread with exact, dated NCSA or SportsRecruits prices was found. The r/baseball and r/CollegeBaseball searches returned only aggregated summaries.
- Most NCSA figures come from sites that sell competing products (NextCommit, GetRecruited, Rawrecruit, RecruitReach, ProductiveRecruit, Streamline Athletes) and may be biased. No independent news article with 2025-2026 NCSA prices was found.
- No baseball-specific secondhand price was found for SportsRecruits; the club data point is field hockey.

## 5. Criticism about price, auto-renewal, refunds, and sales pressure

### Takeaway
NCSA draws the heaviest criticism: high-pressure sales calls, binding contracts with cancellation penalties, and refund difficulty. CaptainU draws repeated Trustpilot and Glassdoor allegations of charges after cancellation and slow refunds, and its terms say there are no refunds. BeRecruited's monthly plan auto-renews by design. No notable billing criticism of FieldLevel or SportsRecruits was found in this research.

### Cited Findings
- **NCSA sales pressure:** a Trustpilot reviewer warned parents about pressure from sales staff. Another said NCSA "will pester you constantly until you sign up for an expensive lifetime membership." A third said NCSA "completely stopped responding" after they asked for a prorated refund. Another called it "absolutely not worth the cost" — [Trustpilot NCSA](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.ncsasports.org); [Trustpilot NCSA p.5](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.ncsasports.org?page=5)
- **NCSA binding contract and cancellation:** a BBB complainant said they were not told that joining was a binding contract and were told they owed 30% of the total when cancelling a week later. They also said mental-health issues, including suicide attempts, were not accepted as valid reasons to cancel. Other complaints say it is hard to reach anyone at NCSA — [BBB NCSA complaints](https://www.bbb.org/us/fl/bradenton/profile/athletic-organizations/national-collegiate-scouting-association-llc-0654-7001121/complaints?page=6)
- **NCSA no refund mid-contract:** one third-party site says a family that signs up for a $3,500 MVP package and decides after four months it is not delivering value cannot cancel for a refund. Single source; attribution among the GetRecruited/NCSA-cost results is unclear — [GetRecruited: How much does NCSA cost](https://getrecruited.cc/how-much-does-ncsa-cost)
- Reviewers say NCSA sets the price during the sales call, and that once past the cancellation window the family owes the full amount whether or not they use the service — [GetRecruited: NCSA worth it](https://getrecruited.cc/is-ncsa-worth-it); [GetRecruited: How much does NCSA cost](https://getrecruited.cc/how-much-does-ncsa-cost)
- **Coach skepticism:** a blog reports that college coaches it spoke with in 2025 "were quick to discount communication from NCSA" — [RecruitReach](https://www.recruitreach.com/blog/is-ncsa-worth-it)

### Inferences
- The most common complaints are binding contracts, opaque pricing, high-pressure sales calls, charges after cancellation, and no refunds. A startup can position directly against these: published prices, monthly cancel-anytime, one-click cancellation, a reminder email before annual renewal, and a clear refund window.
- If NCSA and SportsRecruits really share an owner, the two "competitors" cover both the high-touch and club-bundled segments. The self-serve low-price segment is held by FieldLevel and CaptainU.
