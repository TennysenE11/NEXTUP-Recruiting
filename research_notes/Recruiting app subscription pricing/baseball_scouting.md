# Baseball Scouting, Showcase and Profile Membership Pricing (PBR, Perfect Game, Baseball Factory, others), US HS recruits, 2025-2026

Research method note: WebFetch of www.perfectgame.org and www.prepbaseball.com failed because those hosts are outside this session's allowed network, and per the tool's guidance no further direct page fetches were tried. Every figure below comes from WebSearch result summaries of the cited pages, not from full-page reads. Most official pricing pages are undated. Figures marked "unconfirmed" could not be tied to a clear, dated source. Research date: 2026-11-25.

## Q1. Plan names and prices: monthly, yearly, and annual-discount percent

### Takeaway
Scouting-service subscriptions cluster at about $20-$40/month or $120-$400/year, with annual discounts from about 17% to 50%. PBR's fan/HS tier is $20/mo or $120/yr (50% off). Perfect Game's Crosschecker runs $21.99-$39.99/mo ($264-$399/yr, 17-27% off). Contact-data "scout" tiers run $350-$800/yr. Pure recruiting-outreach apps already sit at $10-$50/mo, which is the most direct comparison for a recruiting-email app.

## Q2. What subscriptions include vs. what costs extra (showcase/event fees and typical ranges)

### Takeaway
Subscriptions mostly buy viewing access (rankings, scouting reports, other players' video, stats and streaming). The player's own profile, PG Grade or PBR evaluation, and verified measurables come from paying for an event. Per-player showcase fees run from free or about $99 (PBR state events) to about $250-$1,000+ (PG national). Team tournament entries ($1,100-$2,300 per team) are a separate cost.

## Q4. Typical total annual spend on baseball recruiting (showcases + subscriptions)

### Takeaway
Showcases alone typically cost a few hundred to about $3,000 a year, plus travel. All-in recruiting-track baseball (travel team, showcases, lessons, travel) is commonly reported at about $3,000-$11,000 a year, and elite or national paths run higher. Most of these figures are vendor estimates or self-reported forum anecdotes, not surveys.

### Cited Findings
- 3-6 showcase or camp events per year cost $600-$3,000, plus travel to attend. — [ProLook: Baseball Recruiting Costs breakdown](https://prolook.app/blog/how-much-does-baseball-recruiting-cost-a-breakdown-for-parents-athletes)
- A typical year lands around $8,500-$11,000+, assuming 3 showcases at $650 each plus about 25 private lessons. — [QuestFit: What Does College Baseball Recruiting Really Cost?](https://questfit.com/athletic-recruiting-costs)
- Most families on a college recruiting track spend $3,000-$10,000/year (NCSA, a recruiting vendor). — [NCSA: How Much Does College Recruiting Cost?](https://www.ncsasports.org/blog/how-much-does-recruiting-cost)
- Exposure spending of $500-$2,000+ per year is added on top of elite team costs. — [Dugout Authority: Real Cost of Elite Youth Baseball](https://www.dugoutauthority.com/post/the-real-cost-of-playing-elite-youth-baseball-national-overview)
- A typical all-in travel baseball year costs $3,000-$8,000: local or regional programs $2,000-$4,000, and elite or showcase programs $8,000-$15,000+ (2026 marketing-site estimate). — [WAR Baseball: How Much Does Travel Baseball Cost? 2026](https://warbaseball.org/travel-baseball-cost/)
- Travel team cost expectations for 2026. — [FundLocker: Travel Baseball Team Costs 2026](https://www.fundlocker.org/blog/travel-baseball-team-costs-2026)
- HSBaseballWeb parents report: about $8K/yr on the "sane" end of travel ball; one junior-year budget of about $2,900 that included a $700 national showcase; showcases at about $250-$300 each; travel and hotels 4-8 times a year at $800-$1,200 per tournament or camp; at the low end, Legion ball at about $75/season. All self-reported and undated or mixed-date. — [HSBBWeb: How much money do you spend for baseball each year?](https://community.hsbaseballweb.com/topic/how-much-money-do-you-spend-for-baseball-on-your-child-each-year); [HSBBWeb: Your baseball budget](https://community.hsbaseballweb.com/topic/your-baseball-budget?nc=1); [HSBBWeb: Budgeting](https://community.hsbaseballweb.com/topic/budgeting?reply=2982475076148613&nc=1); [HSBBWeb: Cost of being recruited](https://community.hsbaseballweb.com/topic/cost-of-being-recruited)
- An HSBBWeb thread titled "Spending $100K on Travel Ball" shows the extreme upper end is discussed. Its contents were not retrieved. — [HSBBWeb: Spending $100K on Travel Ball](https://community.hsbaseballweb.com/topic/spending-100k-on-travel-ball)
- Skeptical framing: Bat Digest says a PG showcase is "probably not" worth it in most cases. GetRecruited advises skipping showcases freshman year and says a PG event is worth it mainly for players with D1 measurables. — [Bat Digest](https://batdigest-static.pages.dev/blog/is-the-perfect-game-worth-it); [GetRecruited](https://getrecruited.college/are-baseball-recruiting-camps-worth-it)

### Inferences
- Families on a recruiting track already spend thousands per year. A $10-$30/mo recruiting-email app ($120-$360/yr) would be about 1-10% of a typical recruiting budget and less than one PG showcase entry. That supports a "cheaper than one showcase" positioning.
- Spending peaks in sophomore through junior years, which suggests yearly or "season" plans of 12-24 months aligned to the recruiting window.

### Gaps
- No independent survey (non-vendor) of baseball-specific recruiting spend from 2025-2026 was found. Figures come from vendor blogs (NCSA, QuestFit, ProLook, WAR Baseball) and forum anecdotes.
- A search summary mentioned a commenter reporting "upwards of $30,000 over three years" of high school summer ball, but the source page could not be identified. UNCONFIRMED; not usable as cited.
- Forum posts are of mixed or unknown dates; several HSBBWeb figures may predate 2020.
