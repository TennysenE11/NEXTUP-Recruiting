# OpenSpot brand files

- `openspot-logo.svg`: the full logo on black. The mark is an open ring (the open spot on a roster) with the purple arrow breaking out through the gap, above the OPENSPOT wordmark.
- `openspot-logo-light.svg`: mark and wordmark side by side, in black and purple, for light backgrounds.
- `openspot-mark.svg`: the mark alone, white and purple, for dark backgrounds.
- Brand purple: `#6c2ee6`. Ink: `#111014`.

The wordmark is built from the Saira typeface (SIL Open Font License), set at its widest and heaviest and slanted. `build_logo.py` regenerates the paths (needs `fonttools`, `brotli` and the `@fontsource-variable/saira` package).

The app icons in `recruiting/` (`icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`) use the mark on black.
