Email queue

Due follow-ups come first, then new schools, best fit and most pitchers leaving first. One tap opens the finished email in Gmail and marks it sent. Press Send in Gmail, come back, and the next one is ready. Changed your mind? Tap Undo.

See the whole queue

X messages

Schools with an X handle from an official page. One tap copies the message and opens the profile. Tap Message, paste, send. When X shows "Seen", mark it here.

Automatic weekly sending

If your weekly scheduled task is set up, it sends due follow-ups and 10 new schools from your saved list every Tuesday and checks your inbox for replies. Import its tracker.json here to see the results.
