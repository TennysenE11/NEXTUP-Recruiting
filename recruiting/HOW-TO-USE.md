# How to use the recruiting tracker

**Open it.** Double-click `recruiting-tracker.html`. It runs in your browser with no install and no internet. Your data saves in that browser only, so use the same browser each time.

**First time.**
1. Open "My profile" and fill in every box: name, height/weight, velo, pitches, GPA, video link, phone, email, high school. The email drafts pull from here.
2. Click "Load the starter list" (or Import, then pick `seed-programs.csv`). You get 40 programs.
3. Rows with a yellow "verify" tag need a check. Open the source link, confirm the coach and email, fix them with Edit, and delete the word "verify" from the notes.

**Emailing a coach.**
1. Click "Draft email". Type one specific line about why this school (a game you watched, their major, a player they developed). Generic lines get ignored.
2. Copy the subject and body and send them from your own email account. The app never sends anything.
3. Back in the app, click "Mark emailed". The status changes to Emailed and a follow-up is set 10 days out.
4. For a more personal draft, click "Copy AI prompt" and paste it into Claude.

**Follow-ups.** The "Due today / overdue" box at the top lists who to follow up with. After you send a follow-up, click "Log follow-up". The next one is set 14 days out. Change any date by hand with Edit. When a coach replies, set Replied to Yes and update the status.

**Back up weekly.** Click Export JSON and keep the file somewhere safe, such as Google Drive. Clearing browser data erases the app's data. To move to another computer or browser, open the app there and Import the JSON. Export CSV opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

**Giving it to a teammate.** Send them the HTML file. Their data stays in their own browser. They should fill in their own profile and replace the starter list with schools that fit them.
