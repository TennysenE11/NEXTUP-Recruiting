# How to use NEXTUP

**Install it as an app.** Open https://tennenneking-bot.github.io/PLUMBER-TEST/recruiting/ on your phone.
- iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
- Android: in Chrome, tap Install app (or menu, then Add to Home screen).

It gets its own icon, opens full screen, and works offline. No App Store and no account. The installed app keeps its own data, separate from the browser, so if you used the app before, Export JSON there first and Import it in the installed app. Teammates install from the same link and keep their own data. You can also download `recruiting-tracker.html` and open it in any browser.

**Free and Pro.** The free plan covers your first 20 schools: profile and profile score, a personal email for each coach, two-tap sending, follow-up reminders, the basic filters (status, division, state, verify), and export and import. Pro ($12.99 a month or $99 a year, launching 2027) adds unlimited schools, the realistic outlook and fit ratings, players leaving at your position, goals and the weekly plan, charts, X messages and every filter. Locked items show a Pro tag; tap it to see what Pro adds. If you have a Pro code, enter it there under "Have a Pro code?". Pro is saved per device, so enter the code once on each phone or computer.

The app has five tabs.

**Home.** Your setup checklist, the number of emails ready to send, goal progress, follow-ups due, and charts: list to offer, reply rate by division, and emails per week. Hover or tap a bar for its numbers, or open "Show as table".

**Profile.** Fill in every box. The Plan card shows whether you're on Free or Pro. The profile score (out of 100) shows what coaches still need from you and what each item is worth. Aim for 85+ before sending. The realistic outlook rates each division Reach, Target or Likely from your fastball, height, handedness and GPA, and checks whether your list is balanced (about 20% reach, 50% target, 30% likely). It's a rough guide: coaches also weigh command, off-speed, projection and video. "My latest news" holds updates like new velo or a showcase date. Every follow-up leads with the newest one.

**Schools.** Each school shows its official athletics logo (829 schools have one; the rest show initials). Tap "Find schools" to add programs from the national list of 1,600 (D1, D2, D3, NAIA, JUCO). Filter by division, state or region, fit for you, has email, has X, and pitchers leaving. In your list, "Filters" offers status, division, fit, state, conference, contact info, outreach stage, follow-up timing, last emailed, pitchers leaving, verify flag and business major. "My states" limits the list and the send queue to the states you pick. Rows tagged "verify" need a look at the official source link before you email.

**Send.** The next email is already written for that coach. Tap "Send in Gmail": the email opens ready to go and the app marks it sent. Press Send in Gmail, come back, and the next one is waiting. Tap Undo if you didn't send it. Follow-ups are set automatically: 10 days after the first email, then every 14 days. X messages work the same way: "Copy and open X", paste, send, then "I sent it". Mark "Seen" when X shows it.

**Goals.** Tap a preset like "Get 10 replies" or set your own number and date. Each goal shows progress and a "How to get there" plan built from your data: how many more schools to contact at your reply rate, follow-ups due, coaches who replied and need an answer, profile items to add, and X messages you can still send. Each step has a button that takes you there. Goals without a date are paced over six weeks. Home shows "This week's plan", which combines all your goals into one to-do list for the week.

**What's tracked, and what isn't.** Replies are found by your weekly email run, or you set them with one tap. Email opens are not tracked: it needs a server and a hidden image, and it's unreliable (Apple Mail fakes opens, and many school email systems block it). X "Seen" is real, so mark it.

**Back up weekly.** Profile tab, Export JSON, save it to Google Drive or iCloud. Import it to restore your data or move it to another device.
